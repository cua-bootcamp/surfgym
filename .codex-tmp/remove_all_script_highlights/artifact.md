## Page system

- US Letter portrait, two pages.
- 1-inch top/bottom and 1.25-inch left/right margins.
- Cambria 12 pt body copy with compact single-line script paragraphs and visible spacing between dialogue branches.
- Centered, bold title; no running header or footer.

## Structural grammar

- Short introductory use note.
- Bold speaker labels at the start of dialogue paragraphs.
- Italic conditional branches and italic bracketed placeholders.
- Dialogue proceeds through unavailable, callback, decline, accept, details, and closing branches.
- Review highlights are distributed across both pages and occur inside otherwise normal or italic text.

- The Gold document removes only the highlight property; text, paragraph order, bold, italic, underline, and pagination are retained.
